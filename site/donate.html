Built in the open. Backed by people.

Unforge started with a simple frustration: building useful software should not mean handing over control or discovering a bill you never expected. If the free app or guides have helped you, you can support that work with an optional tip.

Choose an amount for a one-time tip on Stripe’s hosted payment page. You will see the amount and currency before you pay.

Leave a one-time tip on Stripe →

Entirely optional. No subscription. The app, source code, and guides stay free.

A thank-you, freely given.

A contribution is an optional tip for the free software and documentation already provided by the independent open-source project maintained as Viiberr1128. It does not buy a subscription, additional features, priority support, or a promised release. There are no recurring payments in this contribution flow.

You can use, copy, modify, and share Unforge under its MIT license without donating. We do not offer these contributions as tax-deductible charitable donations.

Please contribute only what feels comfortable. Unforge is a working alpha, with the current capabilities and limitations described in the guide.

Your payment stays separate from your projects.

Checkout takes place on Stripe, after you choose to leave this site. Unforge’s website and local app do not collect card numbers. No payment script is loaded here, and donating does not connect your workspace to a maintainer account.

The maintainer can see payment records that Stripe makes available, such as your name or email if collected, contribution amount, payment status, and limited payment-method details. Your contribution does not add you to a mailing list or a public donor list.

Read our contribution privacy information, contribution terms and refund information, and Stripe’s Privacy Policy before making a payment.

Made a payment mistake or been charged twice? Email support@unforge.app with the payment date, amount, and receipt reference, if available. We will review the payment and refund a confirmed accidental or duplicate contribution to the original payment method. For other refund requests, explain what happened so we can review it. Never send full card details or post payment information in a public issue. Your rights under applicable law remain unchanged.

Money is only one way to help.

Try a disposable project. Tell us where the instructions are confusing. Share a reproducible bug, improve a guide, review a change, or help someone take their first step. Beginners have something valuable to contribute: a fresh view of what should be simpler.
