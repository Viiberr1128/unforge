Free & open source

Make GitHub obsolete.

That’s the goal. Unforge is an open-source GitHub-killer for people who want to own their software workflow — with AI doing the busywork. Today’s alpha covers local projects, isolated lanes, checks, recovery, and observed local publish. An app is not GitHub-free until Unforge can merge, check, and prove the live site without github.com. That gate is computed; it cannot be claimed by turning Actions off.
